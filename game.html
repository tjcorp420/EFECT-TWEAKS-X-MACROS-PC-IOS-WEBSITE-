An EMX Original

Game info

PROJECT EMX is an EMX original built on OG movement and modern assets, heading for a Battle Royale with Build and Zero Build.

What is in the open beta on Roblox today is the core that everything else is built on: build, edit and box fighting. Piece control drills against bots, 1v1 maps made by players, your own map maker, and EMX Bucks earned from real eliminations that unlock skins, pickaxes and effects as you level up. The full Battle Royale match is not in the build yet, so this page keeps what you can play today apart from what is still being made. Nothing below is a promise with a date attached to it.

Playable now

What is in the open beta today. All of it is creative-based: the fighting happens in built spaces rather than across a map.

  • 1v1s

    Straight duels. Build, edit and shoot against one other player.

  • Box fights

    Close-quarters fights inside a box. Fast resets, constant piece control.

  • Zone wars

    A shrinking play space with several players building for position.

  • Speed realistics

    Realistic fights at pace, for players who want the fight rather than the ramp race.

  • Piece control drills

    Practice against bots. Repeat the pieces until the inputs stop being decisions.

  • Player-made maps

    1v1 maps built by players, plus the map maker to build your own.

EMX Bucks are earned by eliminating real players and spend on skins, pickaxes and effects as you level up.
