Keeping it decent

The short version of how the server runs.

  • Bug reports beat complaints

    What you did, what happened, what you expected. That gets fixed; vague frustration does not.

  • No leaks, no exploits

    Found something broken enough to abuse? Report it privately rather than posting it.

  • Never post a licence key

    For EMX software orders use the support centre, not a public channel. Keys and receipts stay private.
