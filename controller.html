BEFORE YOU START

The useful details.

What do I need?

A Windows x64 PC and a supported controller. Initial driver setup may need administrator approval and a Windows restart. Required installers are included. Windows 10 compatibility and additional controller models remain part of release qualification.

Does downloading the ZIP unlock the macros?

No. A valid paid product entitlement is required. Internet access is required for activation and license renewal. Keep your receipt email and transaction ID.

Are the controller bindings universal?

Match Edit and Select to your game’s controller settings. Buttons may have different actions in Build, Edit and Combat modes. EMX does not read the game’s current mode or guarantee that every short pulse will be recognized.

What about Sprint Assist?

Sprint Assist is restricted to forward movement with Double Edit and yields to Drag, manual Edit and R2 reset. It is experimental, off by default, and can occasionally leave Build mode and select a weapon. Disable Sprint Assist if this occurs.

Where do I get help?

Visit EMX Support for activation and setup assistance. Use only the official EMX and Payhip links.
