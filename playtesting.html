How to join

Four steps, none of which involve waiting for approval.

  • Step 01

    Open the experience

    PROJECT EMX runs on Roblox and is free. You need a Roblox account; nothing else is required.

  • Step 02

    Join the Discord

    Sessions are called there, and it is where the people building the game read feedback. Optional, but it is where everything happens first.

  • Step 03

    Play, then say what broke

    Bugs, movement that feels wrong, builds that fight you — that is the useful part. Specifics beat impressions.

  • Step 04

    Watch for the next build

    Changes land on the News page as patch notes, and in Discord as they ship.

What a beta means here

Being honest about this up front saves everyone a bad first session.

  • It changes without warning

    Builds go out mid-week. What worked yesterday may be different today.

  • Empty servers happen

    The live player count on the home page is the real one. If it reads zero, it is zero — bring friends or come to a scheduled session.

  • Progress may be reset

    Levels, unlocks and currency can be wiped while systems are still being built.
