2. License keys are machine-bound

EMX license keys are tied to the machine (hardware) they are activated on. The following can deactivate or invalidate a key:

EMX does not recover, remake, transfer, or re-issue keys for free. If your key becomes invalid for any of the reasons above, a new key requires a new purchase. Please back up your setup and be sure before you reset or reinstall your system.
