HONEST ATTRIBUTION

What this system does—and does not do

EMX owns the tracking

Accounts, referral memory, unique visit counts, approval status, commission records, and the dashboard are handled by EMX.

Checkout confirms a sale

The current product checkout sends EMX a signed completed-sale event. EMX reads its own referral metadata and credits the matching approved account.

No automatic money transfer

The dashboard is an earnings ledger, not a bank. A payout is marked paid only after EMX sends it using the separately agreed method.
